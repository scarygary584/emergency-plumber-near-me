Bathroom Flood Help in Hemet, California

Bathroom flooding can come from a toilet, tub, shower, sink, supply line, or hidden pipe. Stop the closest source first when the area is safe.

Call (951) 621-9532Water shutoff steps

Tell the intake:

  • Which fixture overflowed or leaked
  • Whether water is clean or contaminated
  • Whether water reached walls or lower floors
  • Whether a shutoff valve worked
  • Whether electricity is nearby

Reduce damage safely

1

Stop the fixture

Close the faucet or fixture valve without forcing corroded hardware.

2

Use the main shutoff for uncontrolled flow

Only approach it when the route is dry and safe.

3

Keep away from contaminated water

Do not touch sewage or allow children and pets into the area.

4

Watch ceilings below

Sagging drywall can fall. Do not puncture it or stand underneath it.
