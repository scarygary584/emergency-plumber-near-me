Before reaching into the cabinet

Keep clear of wet outlets, cords, and disposal equipment. Do not disconnect electrical appliances while standing in water. Report uncontrolled flooding immediately when requesting help.

Stop using the sink

Leave the faucet and connected dishwasher off. If a fixture shutoff is safely accessible and you know how it operates, close it gently; never force a stuck or corroded valve. For an uncontrolled supply leak, use the home's main shutoff only if safely accessible.

Supply-line and valve repair

Continuous dripping with the faucet off can indicate a pressurized supply connection or shutoff fault. Tell the provider whether closing the fixture valves changed the flow. A repair may involve the connector, valve, or adjacent piping; visible moisture alone does not identify the failed component.

Drain and trap repair

A leak noticed during draining may involve the trap, a joint, or the drain assembly. Do not run another test that floods the cabinet. Describe any separated parts, corrosion, or recent clog. Ask whether the repair includes checking alignment and testing the connections afterward.

Kitchen plumbing connections

Disposals, dishwasher hoses, and pull-out faucet hoses add possible leak sources. Mention which appliance was operating when the puddle appeared. Photograph visible connections from a safe position rather than dismantling them. For manufacturer-specific guidance, consult Moen's under-cabinet leak guide or your fixture's manual.

Pipe repair or new installation?

A broken pipe does not automatically require replacing every fixture. Ask which part failed, whether neighboring connections are sound, and what replacement installation includes. Plumbing experts should explain the diagnosis before recommending broader changes. Tape or a collection bucket should not be treated as a completed repair.

After the leak stops

Water damage can extend beneath cabinet flooring. Ask whether drying or restoration needs a separate assessment. Keep photographs and repair records. Renters should notify management and confirm who can authorize the visit; see the renter and landlord guide.
