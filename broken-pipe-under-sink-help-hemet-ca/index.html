Cabinet leak request help

Broken Pipe Under Sink Help in Hemet, California

A failed supply connector, angle stop, drain trap, disposal connection, or pipe can quickly soak cabinets and flooring.

Call (951) 621-9532Water shutoff steps

Describe the source:

  • Hot or cold supply line
  • Drain or trap connection
  • Disposal or dishwasher hose
  • Valve that will not close
  • Water near an outlet or cord

Immediate steps

1

Close the fixture valves

Turn them gently clockwise only when accessible. Do not force a damaged valve.

2

Use the main shutoff if flow continues

For an uncontrolled supply leak, the home’s main shutoff may be needed.

3

Avoid wet electrical equipment

Do not reach around a wet disposal, dishwasher cord, or outlet.

4

Empty the cabinet safely

Move chemicals and belongings only after the water and electrical hazards are controlled.
