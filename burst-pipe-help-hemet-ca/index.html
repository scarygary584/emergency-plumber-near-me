Burst Pipe Help in Hemet, California

A burst pipe can move water through walls, ceilings, cabinets, insulation, and flooring within minutes. The first goal is to stop the flow when it is safe to do so.

Call (951) 621-9532Water shutoff steps

Tell the caller intake:

  • Where water is visible
  • Whether it is hot or cold
  • Whether the main valve is off
  • Whether ceilings are sagging
  • Whether outlets or appliances are wet

What to do first

1

Shut off the closest valve

Use a fixture or appliance valve when the source is obvious and the area is safe.

2

Use the main shutoff for major flow

If water is uncontrolled, the main valve may reduce damage. Do not force a corroded valve.

3

Stay clear of electricity

Do not stand in water near outlets, panels, cords, appliances, or powered equipment.

4

Relieve pressure carefully

After water is off, opening a lower cold-water faucet may help drain remaining pressure.

What may have failed?

Possible sources include a split supply pipe, failed flex connector, broken angle stop, frozen or weakened pipe, corroded fitting, appliance line, or water-heater connection. The visible water may be several feet from the actual break.
