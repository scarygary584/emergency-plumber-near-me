Common valve locations

  • Behind a toilet
  • Below a sink
  • At a washing machine
  • Above a water heater
  • At the home’s main supply entry

Which shutoff should you use?

Fixture valve

Use this when the leak is isolated to one toilet, faucet, appliance, or supply line.

Water-heater supply valve

This can stop incoming cold water to the heater when the valve is accessible and safe to operate.

Main house valve

Use the main for uncontrolled water when a smaller valve cannot isolate the source.

Meter-side valve

Some meter valves require a tool or utility access. Do not damage a locked or utility-owned component.

Do not force a stuck valve

Old gate valves and corroded angle stops can break, leak at the stem, or fail internally. If a valve will not move with reasonable hand pressure, stop before turning one plumbing problem into two.

✓ Turn clockwise to close most valves

✓ Use steady hand pressure

✓ Keep clear of electrical hazards

✓ Report a valve that spins or leaks
