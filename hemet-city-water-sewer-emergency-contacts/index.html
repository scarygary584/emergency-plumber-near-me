Private-property problem

When a plumbing request may be appropriate

A problem involving an indoor fixture, appliance connection, private building drain, water heater, wall or slab piping, or the private side of a service line may require a plumbing provider rather than a city crew.

Property ownership and utility boundaries vary. A responding provider or utility may need to inspect before responsibility is clear.

Describe the location

✓ Inside the building or outdoors

✓ Before or after the water meter, if known

✓ One fixture or several fixtures

✓ Clean water or wastewater

✓ Public street, sidewalk, yard, or structure

✓ Whether water is still spreading
