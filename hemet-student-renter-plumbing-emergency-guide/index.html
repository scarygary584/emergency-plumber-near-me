Do not take these risks

  • Do not stand in water near electricity
  • Do not touch sewage with bare skin
  • Do not force a stuck shutoff valve
  • Do not use chemical drain products during a backup
  • Do not enter a locked maintenance area

Fast checklist

What to do in the first few minutes

1

Identify the hazard

Decide whether the problem is clean water, possible sewage, loss of water, or water near electrical equipment.

2

Limit water safely

Use an accessible toilet or sink valve only when it is safe. If you do not know the valve, do not guess or force it.

3

Contact the landlord

Use the lease’s emergency number or resident portal and include photos, the unit, affected fixture, and whether water is spreading.

4

Protect neighboring units

If water may be traveling through a wall or floor, tell management and any immediately affected neighbor without entering an unsafe area.

Campus versus rental

Use the contact responsible for the location

For a plumbing problem in an off-campus Hemet rental, contact the landlord or property manager and request appropriate plumbing help. For an emergency physically on an MSJC campus, follow the college’s official Campus Safety instructions.

MSJC’s official Campus Safety page lists 911 for emergencies and provides current campus-safety contact information. Verify those details directly on the college website.
