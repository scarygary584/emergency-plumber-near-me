Kitchen Sink Overflow Help in Hemet, California

Stop the faucet, dishwasher, and disposal. If water keeps rising or appears in another fixture, the problem may be farther down the drain line.

Call (951) 621-9532Clogged drain help

Check when safe:

  • Faucet supply valves
  • Drain and trap connections
  • Disposal and dishwasher area
  • Cabinet floor and nearby outlets
  • Whether another drain is backing up

Immediate steps

1

Turn off the faucet

Use the fixture valves only when they are accessible and turn freely.

2

Cut power to wet equipment safely

Do not touch a wet disposal, cord, or outlet.

3

Stop dishwasher use

A shared drain problem can force more water into the sink or cabinet.

4

Avoid chemical mixtures

Do not add more drain products when another product may already be present.
