Water rises in the basin

Food debris, grease buildup, or a restriction farther along the drain can slow wastewater. Tell the plumber whether both sides of a double sink fill and whether the dishwasher was running. Water moving between the basins is useful information, but it does not locate the blockage by itself.

Water appears under the sink

A supply connection may leak even with the faucet off. A trap, drain joint, or disposal connection may leak only while water drains. Observe from a dry position without touching wet equipment. For a separated fitting or visible cabinet leak, see broken pipe under sink help.

Other fixtures back up too

If a nearby shower, toilet, or another sink is also affected, stop household water use and report all affected fixtures. A shared drain or sewer restriction may need a different assessment from a single kitchen clog. See sewer backup guidance when wastewater appears elsewhere.

What to avoid while the kitchen drain is blocked

Do not keep running the disposal or dishwasher to test whether the water will clear. Do not reach inside the disposal or remove a trap full of backed-up water. Avoid adding drain chemicals, especially when a product is already present. Tell the attending plumber what you used and when; keep the product container available without handling contaminated water.

Keep children and pets away from the spill. Do not touch a wet switch, outlet, appliance plug, or power cord. Hemet kitchen sink overflow questions

Why does the sink fill when the dishwasher drains?

The dishwasher may discharge through a connection shared with the sink. A restriction along that route can cause water to back up. Stop the cycle and tell the plumber when the backup occurs rather than repeatedly testing the appliance.

Can a leaking kitchen sink wait until morning?

That depends on whether the water is fully stopped, contamination is present, and damage is continuing. Uncontrolled flow or water near electrical equipment needs prompt attention. Describe the conditions when requesting help and confirm availability directly.

Will clearing the drain repair a leaking pipe?

Not necessarily. Clearing a blockage restores flow, while a damaged or disconnected fitting may need a separate repair. Ask the provider to check both the drainage problem and the visible leak before the job is considered complete.
