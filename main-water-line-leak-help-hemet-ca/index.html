Underground or service-line leak help

Main Water Line Leak Help in Hemet, California

A main-line leak can show up as unexplained wet ground, low pressure, a spinning meter, or water surfacing near the home or street.

Call (951) 621-9532Water shutoff steps

Report these clues:

  • Where water is surfacing
  • Whether pressure dropped
  • Whether the meter moves with fixtures off
  • Whether the leak is before or after the meter
  • Any road, sidewalk, or foundation impact

What to do first

1

Stop indoor water use

Turn off faucets, irrigation, appliances, and fixtures so meter movement is easier to interpret.

2

Check the meter only when safe

Do not enter traffic, flooded vaults, or unstable ground.

3

Use the customer shutoff if accessible

Do not force a seized valve or disturb utility-owned equipment.

4

Document the wet area

Note growth, location, and whether the water is clear or contaminated.
