Water heater problem help

No Hot Water Help in Hemet, California

A sudden loss of hot water may involve power, gas supply, a failed heating component, thermostat trouble, sediment, or a leaking tank. Avoid opening panels or relighting equipment unless the manufacturer’s instructions clearly permit it.

Have these details ready:

  • Gas or electric water heater
  • Tank or tankless system
  • Age and model when known
  • Any error code, leak, odor, or noise
  • Whether power or gas is available elsewhere

Safe checks before requesting help

1

Check whether the problem is isolated

Test more than one hot-water fixture so you know whether the issue is one faucet or the whole home.

2

Look for leaks

Check the floor and visible connections without touching hot surfaces or electrical components.

3

Note lights or error codes

Record what the display shows. Do not remove covers or bypass safety controls.

4

Leave gas concerns alone

If you smell gas, leave the area and contact the gas utility or emergency services from a safe location.

When it may be urgent

No hot water alone may be schedulable, but active leaking, scorched wiring, a gas odor, popping with water discharge, or water near electrical equipment deserves faster attention.

✓ Do not repeatedly reset a tripping breaker

✓ Do not seal a temperature-pressure relief valve

✓ Keep children away from leaking or overheated equipment

✓ Photograph labels and error codes when safe
