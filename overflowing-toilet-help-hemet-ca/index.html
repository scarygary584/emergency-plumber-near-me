First steps during a plumbing emergency

The small supply valve is usually behind or beside the bowl. A traditional multi-turn handle normally closes clockwise; a quarter-turn model only moves through a short arc. Cleanup and preventing another backup

Restoring drainage does not make contaminated flooring or belongings clean. Keep the affected space restricted and avoid direct contact with wastewater.
