Toilet overflow help

Overflowing Toilet Help in Hemet, California

When the bowl keeps rising, stop the water before trying anything else. Repeated flushing can turn a clog into floor damage.

Call (951) 621-9532Sewer backup signs

Do this first

  • Do not flush again
  • Close the toilet supply valve
  • Lift the tank lid only if safe
  • Keep towels away from sewage
  • Stop other fixture use if drains are gurgling

How to stop the bowl from rising

1

Turn the valve clockwise

The small valve behind or beside the toilet controls water entering the tank. Do not force it if it is badly corroded.

2

Stop the tank refill

If the valve cannot be reached quickly, lifting the float in the tank may temporarily stop refilling.

3

Do not use nearby drains

If a tub, shower, or sink also gurgles or backs up, the problem may be farther down the drain system.

4

Call with the pattern

Report whether only one toilet is affected or several fixtures are involved.

Fixture clog or sewer backup?

A single toilet that overflows after flushing may have a local trap or branch clog. Multiple slow fixtures, wastewater in a tub, or repeated backups at the lowest drain may indicate a main-line problem.
