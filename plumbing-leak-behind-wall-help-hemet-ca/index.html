Concealed plumbing leak help

Plumbing Leak Behind Wall Help in Hemet, California

Water stains, soft drywall, peeling paint, musty odors, or running-water sounds can point to a concealed leak. The visible damage may not be directly below the source.

Call (951) 621-9532Water shutoff steps

Report these details:

  • Where staining or moisture appears
  • Which fixtures are nearby
  • Whether the meter moves with water off
  • Whether ceilings are sagging
  • Whether outlets or switches are wet

What to do first

1

Stop the likely fixture

Avoid using nearby sinks, tubs, toilets, appliances, or upstairs fixtures.

2

Shut off water for active flow

Use a fixture or main valve only when safe and accessible.

3

Stay clear of electricity

Do not touch wet switches, outlets, fixtures, or wiring.

4

Do not cut the wall open

Hidden pipes and wiring should be located before opening finished surfaces.
