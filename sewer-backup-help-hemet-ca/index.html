Sewer Backup Help in Hemet, California

Water or sewage rising from a toilet, shower, tub, floor drain, or cleanout can signal a blocked building drain or sewer line. Stop adding water to the system.

Stop using:

  • Toilets
  • Showers and tubs
  • Sinks
  • Dishwashers
  • Washing machines

Protect people and limit the mess

1

Keep people and pets away

Wastewater may contain bacteria and other contaminants. Avoid direct contact.

2

Stop all water use

Every flush or drain can add more water behind the blockage.

3

Do not mix chemicals

Drain chemicals can create fumes, splashing hazards, and additional risks for whoever opens the line.

4

Describe where it appeared first

The lowest fixture often provides a useful clue about where the restriction may be.

Signs the problem may be in the main drain

More than one fixture backing up, gurgling toilets, water rising in a tub when another fixture runs, or wastewater appearing at the lowest drain can point beyond a single fixture trap.

✓ Note which fixtures are affected

✓ Note whether neighbors have a similar problem

✓ Locate the exterior cleanout if known
