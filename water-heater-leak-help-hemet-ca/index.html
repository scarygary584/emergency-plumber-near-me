When to request emergency plumbing

  • Liquid is spreading into occupied rooms.
  • The tank appears split or heavily corroded.
  • Moisture has reached wiring or controls.
  • The relief outlet is discharging continuously.

If you smell gas, leave immediately. Contact the gas utility from a safe location; call 911 for immediate danger. Do not wait for a service connection through this website.

First steps for a water heater leak

Do not remove access covers, touch wet electrical parts, relight a burner or operate controls while standing in a puddle. Keep children and pets away. Follow the unit's manufacturer instructions for shutting it down only if you can do so safely from a dry location. If the controls are unfamiliar or inaccessible, ask for professional assistance.

A clearly identified cold supply shutoff valve may limit incoming flow if it is safely accessible. Do not force a stuck handle. Stopping incoming flow does not empty the tank or make it safe to work on. Keep the heating equipment off until a qualified technician confirms it can be returned to service.

Never cap or plug a temperature-and-pressure relief outlet. Its discharge may be dangerously hot, and the cause needs assessment. For suspected gas trouble, follow SoCalGas guidance: leave the area and contact the utility from a safe location without operating switches or creating an ignition source.

What to tell a Hemet plumbing provider

Describe what you can see without taking equipment apart. A photo from a dry, safe position may help explain the location, but it cannot confirm a diagnosis. Note whether moisture starts at the top, around a lower valve, beneath the cabinet or at a nearby line. Liquid can travel down the outside before collecting on the floor.

Connections and fittings

Report visible dripping around accessible connections. A fitting problem may allow a targeted repair instead of replacing the whole unit.

Tank and lower valve

A wet base can have more than one cause. Tell the technician whether you see corrosion or a trail from higher up; do not assume the tank is the source.

Heating behavior

Mention whether the unit stopped heating, made unusual sounds or showed an error code before you shut it down. Read a display only if safely visible.

Also note the approximate age, brand and model if known. For tankless or heat-pump water heaters, identify the equipment type when requesting assistance so the provider can confirm whether they work on that system.

Repair options and water heater installation

The inspection should establish where the leak originates before you authorize a solution. Ask the technician to explain whether the issue involves a serviceable component, an external plumbing connection or the storage tank itself. Have them distinguish the immediate repair from any additional work they recommend.

External leaks may be addressed through component repairs when the rest of the system remains suitable for use. A confirmed internal tank failure generally requires replacement. Ask what evidence supports the recommendation and whether the existing manufacturer's warranty applies. Keep the model and serial number available for that discussion.

If replacement is proposed, discuss household demand, equipment dimensions, access and the existing energy source. Changing equipment types may require additional work beyond exchanging the unit. Confirm who handles any required permits, removal of the old equipment and startup checks. A new installation should be explained as a separate scope rather than assumed to be included in the initial visit.

Ask what the repair services include before giving approval: diagnosis, parts, labor and follow-up checks may be separate items. The provider should explain the proposed work and obtain authorization. This intake website does not diagnose equipment or provide estimates.
