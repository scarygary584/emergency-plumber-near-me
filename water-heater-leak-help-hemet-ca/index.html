Leaking water heater help

Water Heater Leak Help in Hemet, California

Water around a heater can come from a supply line, valve, fitting, drain valve, relief pipe, or the tank itself. Keep clear of electrical and gas hazards.

Call (951) 621-9532Water shutoff steps

Call promptly when:

  • Water is spreading quickly
  • The tank appears split or rusted through
  • Water is near wiring or controls
  • You smell gas
  • The relief pipe is discharging continuously

What you can check without taking equipment apart

Top connections

Look for water around the hot and cold supply connections and nearby fittings.

Drain valve

Water near the lower drain valve may come from the valve itself or may have traveled down the tank.

Relief pipe

Occasional discharge and continuous flow are different clues. Do not cap or plug a relief pipe.

Tank body

Water emerging from the tank shell or base can indicate internal corrosion or tank failure.

Safety comes before diagnosis

Do not remove covers, touch wet electrical parts, relight equipment, or operate controls while standing in water. For a suspected gas leak, leave the area and contact the gas utility or emergency services from a safe location.
